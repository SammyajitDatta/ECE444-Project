## Coordination

### Weekly Meetings

The team will hold one mandatory meeting each week on **Wednesday** from **1:00 - 2:00 pm** at **Bahen Centre for Information Technology**. The meeting takes place right after the mandatory in-person ECE444 lecture, so each person is expected to be able to attend in person. In events of there being no usable space in Bahen, the meeting can be relocated to other available spaces depending on what is available. 


Meetings may be used for:

- Reviewing progress since the previous meeting
- Working collaboratively on implementation or debugging
- Reviewing pull requests and integration work
- Discussing design or implementation decisions
- Updating GitHub Issues and internal priorities
- Planning the next set of development tasks

Additional urgent meetings may be scheduled when necessary to address blockers or approaching deadlines. These meetings will only be added if both members agree.

Both team members are expected to attend scheduled meetings. If a member cannot attend, they must inform the other member as early as reasonably possible and offer an alternative meeting time.

### Meeting Minutes

Meeting minutes will be recorded during each team meeting and stored in the project repository.

Responsibility for recording meeting minutes will alternate between team members. Meeting minutes will include:

- Date and time of the meeting
- Members in attendance
- Topics discussed
- Decisions made
- Tasks assigned to each member
- Upcoming deadlines
- Unresolved issues or blockers

Both team members will review the meeting minutes to ensure that they accurately reflect the discussion and decisions made.

### Communication

The team's primary communication methods outside of meetings will be **Discord and iMessage**.

Discord will primarily be used for project-related discussions, technical questions, links, files, GitHub references, and development updates. iMessage may be used for quick communication, meeting coordination, and urgent matters.

Team members are expected to regularly check project messages and respond within **24 hours** when a response is required.

For urgent matters involving an approaching deadline or an issue blocking another team member's work, members should respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Important decisions made through Discord or iMessage will be recorded in the meeting minutes or the relevant GitHub Issue so that significant decisions remain documented.

### Task Management

The team will use **GitHub Issues** to organize and track project work. Development tasks will be divided into reasonably sized issues with a clear description, expected outcome, owner, and target completion period.

Team members will review the open issues regularly during meetings to ensure that work is progressing and that tasks are distributed appropriately.

Development work will normally be completed on separate Git branches associated with the relevant issue. Changes will be submitted through **Pull Requests** and reviewed and tested by the other team member before being merged into the main branch.

## Conflict Resolution

### Create/Technical Disagreements

Team members will first attempt to resolve disagreements through discussion. Each member will explain their reasoning and the advantages and disadvantages of their proposed approach.

For technical or product disagreements, the team will use objective evidence whenever possible, including:

- Project requirements
- User needs
- Testing results
- Technical feasibility
- Implementation complexity
- Course requirements

If necessary, the team may create a small prototype or experiment to compare proposed solutions.

Because Team Blue consists of two members, a majority vote cannot resolve a disagreement when the members disagree. If a significant disagreement cannot be resolved after discussion and evaluation of available evidence, the team will consult the course teaching staff for guidance.

### Missed Deadlines

Team members should notify the other member as early as possible if they believe they will be unable to complete an assigned task by its agreed target date.

When reasonable notice is provided, the team may:

- Adjust the target date
- Redistribute part of the task
- Reduce or modify the task's scope where appropriate

For an **unexcused missed agreed deadline**, the following process will be used:

1. **First occurrence:** The missed deadline will be discussed between both members. The responsible member will explain the reason for the delay, provide a new completion time, and the incident will be recorded in the meeting minutes.
2. **Second occurrence:** The team will hold a dedicated discussion to determine why deadlines are repeatedly being missed and create a specific corrective plan. Tasks may be redistributed if necessary.
3. **Third repeated occurrence:** If a member repeatedly misses agreed deadlines without reasonable notice or justification, the issue will be documented and brought to the attention of the course teaching staff. Relevant evidence, such as GitHub activity, task assignments, meeting minutes, and team communications, may be provided.

A missed deadline caused by a legitimate emergency or other reasonable extenuating circumstance will be handled collaboratively and will not automatically count toward this escalation process. Details for what is classified as such can be found [here](https://undergrad.engineering.utoronto.ca/petitions/about-petitions/#extenuating-circumstances).

### Incomplete or Unsatisfactory Work

If submitted work does not meet the team's agreed requirements, the reviewing team member will provide specific feedback explaining what needs to be corrected.

The member responsible for the work will be given a reasonable opportunity to revise it before the other member takes over the task.

If incomplete or inadequate work repeatedly prevents the team from meeting project requirements, the issue will be handled using the same escalation process as repeated missed deadlines.

### Exceptional Contributions

The team recognizes that the amount of work required for individual tasks may vary throughout the project.

If one member makes an exceptional contribution during a particular milestone, the contribution will be acknowledged by the team and considered when distributing future work. Where appropriate, upcoming tasks may be adjusted to keep the overall workload balanced.

Exceptional contributions do not remove the expectation that both members contribute equally overall to writing, editing, rewriting, presenting, development, and team coordination.
